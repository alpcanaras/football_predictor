FOOTBALL PREDICTOR
==================

To install
  1. Drag "Football Predictor" onto the "Applications" folder.
  2. Open it from Applications.

If macOS says it cannot verify the app
  This app is not signed by an Apple developer account. macOS shows this
  message for every such app. It is safe to open the app if you got it from
  the person who shared it with you. Do ONE of these:

  A. System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down. Next to
     "Football Predictor was blocked" click "Open Anyway".
  B. Or open the Terminal app and run this line, then open the app again:
         xattr -cr "/Applications/Football Predictor.app"

What you need
  A Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer), macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer, and an
  internet connection for fixtures and new results.

How it works
  The app opens a page in your browser. It downloads the match list and
  results from football-data.co.uk by itself, and keeps its data in
      ~/Library/Application Support/Football Predictor
  To remove everything, drag the app to the Bin and delete that folder.

  When you close the browser page the app quits by itself after ten minutes.
  To quit sooner use Data > Quit in the page.

The numbers are estimates. They do not guarantee a result.
